# Refunds and cancellations

## Self-serve cancellation

Cancel under **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. The workspace stays on the paid plan
until the end of the paid period, then reverts to the free tier. Data is retained for 90
days after downgrade.

## Refund eligibility

- **Duplicate or double charges** are refunded in full, always. No approval needed.
- **Annual plans** are refundable in full within **14 days** of the initial charge or
  renewal. After 14 days, annual plans are non-refundable for the remainder of the term.
- **Monthly plans** are not refundable after the charge posts; cancellation stops the next
  charge.
- **Failed-renewal reactivations** are billed from the reactivation date, not backdated.

## Exceptions

Refunds outside this policy — including partial-term annual refunds after day 14, goodwill
credits, and refunds on accounts with an active order form — require **manager approval**.
Support agents cannot approve these unilaterally.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method and take 5-10 business days to appear.
Northwind cannot refund to a different card or bank account.
