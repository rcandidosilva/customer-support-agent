# Incidents and SLA

Live status and history: `status.northwind-analytics.com`. Subscribe there for updates;
Support does not send individual incident notifications.

## Uptime commitment

| Plan       | Monthly uptime SLA | Credit for a miss |
|------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Starter    | none (best effort) | none              |
| Team       | none (best effort) | none              |
| Business   | 99.5%              | 10% of monthly fee |
| Enterprise | 99.9%              | Per order form, up to 30% |

SLA credits are **not automatic**. The customer must request a credit within 30 days of the
incident, referencing the incident ID from the status page. Credits are applied to a future
invoice and are never paid in cash.

Eligibility determinations and any credit above the standard percentage require the account
manager and finance — never the front-line agent.

## Postmortems

Public postmortems are published on the status page within 5 business days for Sev-1
incidents. Customer-specific root-cause letters are available to Enterprise on request via
the account manager.
